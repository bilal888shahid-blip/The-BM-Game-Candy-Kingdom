Welcome to The BM Game – Candy Kingdom!

Enter the colorful world of Candy Kingdom, an exciting match-3 puzzle game created by Bali. Match three or more colorful candies, complete challenging objectives and explore an adventure filled with fun.

How to Play

Swipe adjacent candies to match three or more candies of the same color. Complete the target before your available moves run out.
